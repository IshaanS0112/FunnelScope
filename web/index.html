Business question — Where in the purchase journey do shoppers leave?

Ordered funnel — users reaching each stage, in sequence

    05

    Business question — What is fixing each step worth?

    Recoverable revenue at a 5-point lift, upper bound with 95% interval

    Model: min(users entering × lift, users lost) × P(purchase | next stage) × AOV. The lift applies to users entering a step — the denominator of the rate it changes. Every figure is an upper bound: it assumes recovered shoppers buy like those who completed the step unaided. The interval covers sampling noise only, not that assumption.

    06

    Business question — Given limited engineering time, what should we do first?

    RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Reach is measured from the funnel; impact, confidence and effort are judgement — stated with the assumption behind each, so a reader who disagrees can change one number and watch the ranking move.
